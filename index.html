Yume means dream.

We're a New Jersey family of restaurants, and ramen is the bowl we grew up chasing — the one you think about on the drive home. Getting here

Freehold — downtown on West Main Street; street parking and the municipal lots on Court Street.

Quaker Bridge — inside Quaker Bridge Mall off US-1, Lawrence Township; free mall parking, enter by the center court.

Food allergy notice

Our kitchen handles wheat (gluten), soy, egg, milk, fish, shellfish, sesame, peanuts and tree nuts, and dishes are prepared in shared equipment and fryers. We can't guarantee any item is free of any allergen, including items marked gluten-free or vegan. Tell us about any allergy before ordering and we'll do our best; cross-contact is possible. Consuming raw or undercooked eggs, seafood or meat may increase your risk of foodborne illness.
