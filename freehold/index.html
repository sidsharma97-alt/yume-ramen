Good to know

Where is Yume Ramen in Freehold?

At 5 West Main Street in downtown Freehold, NJ 07728.

Does Yume Ramen Freehold have vegetarian or vegan ramen?

Yes. Yasai Karamiso and Mori Curry are made with vegetarian dashi, and any bowl can be made vegan by swapping to kale noodles. Our kitchen uses shared equipment, so tell us about any allergy before you order.

Is there a gluten-free option?

You can swap noodles for rice in any bowl. Our kitchen handles wheat and uses shared fryers, so we cannot guarantee any dish is free of gluten.

Can I order Yume Ramen Freehold online?

Yes. Order pickup online through our Clover ordering page, pay online and collect at the counter.

Where do I park?

There is street parking on West Main Street and municipal parking on Court Street.

Food allergy notice: our kitchen handles wheat (gluten), soy, egg, milk, fish, shellfish, sesame, peanuts and tree nuts, and dishes are prepared in shared equipment and fryers. We can't guarantee any item is free of any allergen. Consuming raw or undercooked eggs, seafood or meat may increase your risk of foodborne illness.
